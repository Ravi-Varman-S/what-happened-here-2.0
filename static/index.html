What it heard

How it works

1Load & clean

Decode (ffmpeg fallback for stubborn formats), downmix to mono, resample to 16 kHz, normalise to −26 dB RMS behind a peak ceiling.

2Detect, by hand

25 ms frames every 10 ms → RMS → dB. A rolling 25th percentile over 5 s estimates the ambient floor; +6 dB opens an event, +4 dB closes it, and the level must hold 0.10 s either way.

3Label

YAMNet runs once over the file; each event takes the mean score of the windows inside it. Silence, Noise and Static are ruled out — the detector already proved sound is there.

4Report

A labelled spectrogram with the threshold curves underneath, plus a timing table, CSV, summary counts and speech coverage.
